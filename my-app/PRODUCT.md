# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: owners of Indonesian UMKM and small local businesses looking for a
freelance web developer who can take a site from design to deployment, fast and
affordably. Secondary, emerging: small businesses in Australia. Both groups land
here to decide one thing quickly — "can I trust this person to build my site,
and will it look good?" They are not technical; they judge on what they see.

## Product Purpose

Bryan Jacquellino's personal portfolio. It exists to convert a casual visitor
into a freelance inquiry (WhatsApp / email). Success = the visitor scrolls,
believes the work is genuinely high quality, and reaches out. The Projects
section is the proof — it has to make the work look real and impressive, not
described.

## Brand Personality

Bold and energetic. Confident, expressive, alive — motion, depth, and contrast
carry the feel. Three words: bold, kinetic, crafted. The interface itself is a
work sample: it must look like it was built by someone with strong taste. It
should feel premium and a little daring, never timid or corporate-safe.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated portfolios: identical icon + heading + paragraph card
  grids, the hero-metric template, gradient text.
- Template-y SaaS marketing pages with no point of view.
- Static, lifeless project lists where the work is only described, never shown.
- Overly minimal "designer" portfolios that are so quiet they read as empty.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell. The work must be visible and previewable, not summarized.
- The site is the portfolio. Every interaction is a demonstration of craft.
- Earn boldness with control. High energy, but never chaotic or hard to read.
- One clear path to "get in touch." Impress, then make contact effortless.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: sufficient contrast in both light and dark themes, visible
focus states, keyboard-operable carousels and controls. Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` — the site is motion-heavy, so reduced-motion users
must get a calm, fully functional fallback. Bilingual audience (Indonesian and
English); copy stays simple and concrete.
